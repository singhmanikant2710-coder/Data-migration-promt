1. Monthly PD/LGD Grade Change Reconciliation (highest impact)
You mentioned the monthly follow-up to confirm PD/LGD grade changes in the loan system takes time every month. CASRR already has most of what is needed:
- Our review results: CAS PD/LGD vs Bank PD/LGD, Scorecard Assessment selections, and Scorecard Management PD/LGD findings.
- The latest loan data: the monthly Data Mart upload already includes the current PD and LGD grades (and prior month values).

The idea: once the new Data Mart is uploaded, CASRR automatically compares the prior month's review results against the latest loan data and produces a reconciliation report:
- Reconciled – the loan system now reflects the expected PD/LGD change.
- Not yet updated – the expected change is still missing (with RM/PM, review, and account details).
- Needs review – cases the system cannot reconcile automatically.
Your Admin would then only confirm the exceptions, instead of checking every borrower manually. This would be the "first tier of reconcilement" you described, built directly into CASRR rather than as a separate tool.

2. CRM Component KRI Tolerance Monitoring (for the 2027 KRIs)
Since Finding tolerances will be introduced for each CRM component in 2027 (Risk Recognition, Scorecard Management, Underwriting, Credit Servicing, Loan Administration), CASRR could show, by sample, unit and market:
- The Finding/Unsat rate per CRM component against its tolerance (e.g. 5% for Unsat transactions).
- A clear flag when a tolerance is breached, so the team knows immediately when an Issue needs to be raised in the GRC system.
This would build on the Reports page we created together and could later feed the Power BI dashboards.

Both ideas respect your current process – no RM/PM access to CASRR, and Issues continue to be managed in the GRC system.
