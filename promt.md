Unambiguous formats are ones where the month is spelled out as text instead of a number, so there's no way to misread it regardless of regional settings — for example 04-Mar-2026 or Mar 04, 2026. Another option is the ISO format 2026-03-04 (year first), which is internationally understood to always mean year-month-day, so it also can't be misread. Since Geoff confirmed the data is US-only, we don't need either of these — going straight with MM-DD-YYYY as he requested.
